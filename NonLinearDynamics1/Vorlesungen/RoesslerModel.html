14 The Rössler model

The x–y subsystem is a damped oscillator about an unstable focus. The z-equation supplies the folding: $z$ shoots up (a "bump"), driving $x$ down and $z$ back down. Taking a section at constant $y$ reveals a unimodal return map.

$\dot{x} = -y - z, \quad \dot{y} = x + ay, \quad \dot{z} = b + z(x - c)$
