The Lorenz Attractor

Sensitive dependence on initial conditions

The attractor exhibits chaotic behavior. If \( \boldsymbol{x}(t) \) is on the attractor and \( \boldsymbol{x}(t) + \boldsymbol{\delta}(t) \) is a nearby point, then the distance grows exponentially:

\( \| \boldsymbol{\delta}(t) \| \approx \| \boldsymbol{\delta}_0 \| e^{\lambda t} \)

where \( \lambda \approx 0.9 \) is the maximal Lyapunov exponent. This exponential divergence makes long-term prediction impossible.

The prediction horizon scales logarithmically with initial precision:

\( t_{\text{horizon}} \sim \mathcal{O} \left( \frac{1}{\lambda} \ln \frac{a}{\| \boldsymbol{\delta}_0 \|} \right) \)
