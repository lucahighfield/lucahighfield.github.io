Turing Patterns

Interactive visualization of a two-component activator-inhibitor reaction-diffusion system. Adjust parameters to explore pattern formation where the inhibitor diffuses faster than the activator (d > d_c).

∂tu = γ(a − u + u²v) + Δu
∂tv = γ(b − u²v) + dΔv
  • a: Base production rate of the activator (u).
  • b: Base production rate of the inhibitor (v).
  • d: Diffusion ratio (Dv/Du). Must be sufficiently large for patterns to form.
  • γ: Reaction rate multiplier (effectively sets the domain size squared).
